# CEPH MPH Foundational Competency D2-17

Framework: CEPH Accreditation Criteria, MPH foundational competencies (criterion D2)
Domain: Leadership
Competency 17: Apply negotiation and mediation skills to address organizational or community challenges

CEPH interpretation: Students need to know some ways to negotiate/mediate when another party has conflicting interests and/or different desired outcomes from their own and there is a need to come to a common conclusion. Such skills extend beyond the level of negotiating required in a successful intra-group process and assessment must involve more than persuasive communication. It is not sufficient to rely on students’ prior experience or personal knowledge of negotiation and mediation to guide the assessment.
