# CEPH MPH Foundational Competency D2-6

Framework: CEPH Accreditation Criteria, MPH foundational competencies (criterion D2)
Domain: Public Health & Health Care Systems
Competency 6: Discuss the means by which structural bias, social inequities and racism undermine health and create challenges to achieving health equity at organizational, community, and systemic levels

CEPH interpretation: Students should be able to discuss factors (including racism, specifically) that impact health equity at multiple levels for a particular health problem. Students should be able to discuss health disparities and differences among groups, as well as the ways in which organizations, systems, and structures operate that may have inequitable influences on certain groups.
