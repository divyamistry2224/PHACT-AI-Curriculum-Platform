# CEPH MPH Foundational Competency D2-11

Framework: CEPH Accreditation Criteria, MPH foundational competencies (criterion D2)
Domain: Planning & Management to Promote Health
Competency 11: Select methods to evaluate public health programs

CEPH interpretation: “Select” = choose among methods. Students should be able to consider a public health program and choose the appropriate evaluation method for the program. Types of evaluations may include formative evaluation (feasibility, appropriateness, acceptability), process/implementation evaluation (have activities been implemented as intended), outcome/effectiveness evaluation (effect in the target population), and impact evaluation (success in achieving ultimate program goals). Students do not have to actually evaluate, but must be able to identify the correct approach.
