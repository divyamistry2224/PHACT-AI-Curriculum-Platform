# CEPH MPH Foundational Competency D2-15

Framework: CEPH Accreditation Criteria, MPH foundational competencies (criterion D2)
Domain: Policy in Public Health
Competency 15: Evaluate policies for their impact on public health and health equity

CEPH interpretation: Assessment should focus on the evaluation of policies rather than the development of policies. Students should consider how groups are affected by policies, including both intended and unintended consequences with a focus on the impacts on equity.
