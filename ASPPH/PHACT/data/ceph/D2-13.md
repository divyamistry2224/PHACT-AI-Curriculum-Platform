# CEPH MPH Foundational Competency D2-13

Framework: CEPH Accreditation Criteria, MPH foundational competencies (criterion D2)
Domain: Policy in Public Health
Competency 13: Propose strategies to identify relevant communities and individuals and build coalitions and partnerships for influencing public health outcomes

CEPH interpretation: Students should recognize the importance of community involvement and buy-in as instrumental to promoting community change and improvement and should think about how to bring relevant communities and individuals together. Preparation and assessment should emphasize elements of engagement with relevant communities and individuals.
