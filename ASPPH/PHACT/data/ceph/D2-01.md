# CEPH MPH Foundational Competency D2-1

Framework: CEPH Accreditation Criteria, MPH foundational competencies (criterion D2)
Domain: Evidence-based Approaches to Public Health
Competency 1: Apply epidemiological methods to settings and situations in public health practice

CEPH interpretation: Assessment must include the application of various epidemiological study designs (e.g., cohort study) and principles (e.g., sample size, incidence, prevalence, morbidity, etc.). A single setting/situation is insufficient.
