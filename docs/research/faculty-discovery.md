# Faculty discovery interview and initial insights

Target participants: public health faculty, curriculum developers, ASPPH education staff. Duration: 15–20 minutes. Structured interview to understand workflows, pain points, AI adoption barriers, and the potential value of PHACT. This is a research instrument, not completed interview results.

## Opening (1 minute)

Thank you for taking the time to speak with us. We’re developing PHACT, an AI-powered tool designed to support public health curriculum development and review. Before building more features, we’d like to understand how educators currently work, what challenges they face, and what would make an AI-assisted tool useful. There are no right or wrong answers. We’re especially interested in actual experiences, including concerns or reasons you might not use the tool. Would you be comfortable if we took notes?

## Current workflow (3 minutes)

1. How do you currently review, update, or develop your public health course materials? What resources, guidelines, or tools do you use? How frequently do you update your curriculum?
2. What is the most time-consuming or frustrating part? Describe a recent example. Approximately how long did it take?

## AI experience and adoption (4 minutes)

3. Have you used AI tools such as ChatGPT for curriculum development or teaching? If yes, what worked and what didn’t? If no, what prevented you?
4. What concerns would make you hesitant to use a tool that analyzes course materials and recommends improvements? Explore accuracy, trust, privacy, autonomy, workload, and integration only after the participant answers without prompting.

## Trust and requirements (4 minutes)

5. What evidence would you need before trusting a recommendation? Would authoritative links help? Would you want to review and approve every recommendation?
6. At what stage of your workflow could PHACT help: syllabus review, gap identification, competency comparison, or course improvement recommendations?
7. What would make it difficult, inconvenient, or risky to adopt? What would need to change for regular use?

## Value and validation (3 minutes)

8. If PHACT could solve one problem, what should it be?
9. How would you determine usefulness: time saved, recommendation quality, competency alignment, or ease of implementation?
10. Would you test an early prototype? What would you want demonstrated first?

## Closing (1 minute)

Thank you. Your feedback will help us prioritize real needs. Is there anything about curriculum development or AI adoption we haven’t asked?

## Initial stakeholder observations

Organizational leadership indicated varied faculty AI acceptance, concerns about student critical thinking, and distrust caused by fabricated citations or irrelevant sources. Direct faculty validation remains pending. No evidence established a relationship between age and AI acceptance.

## Source inspection hypothesis

Let faculty open the cited passage and original reference, check organization/version/framework, and distinguish source evidence from AI interpretation. Hypothesis: source inspection helps faculty judge trustworthiness and applicability. This remains a proposed feature and a hypothesis to test.
