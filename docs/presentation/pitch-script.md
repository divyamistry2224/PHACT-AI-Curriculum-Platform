# PHACT pitch script

User-supplied presentation narrative, retained as presentation material. Workforce figures and impact projections were not independently verified in the packaging review.

Think about the public health challenges that have shaped our nation and world in recent history, from the September 11 attacks, which tested our emergency preparedness and response systems, to Hurricane Katrina, which devastated communities and exposed critical gaps in disaster response and health equity, to the COVID-19 pandemic, which disrupted lives, separated families, and affected communities across the globe.

Each reminds us of the vital role public health plays in protecting lives, strengthening communities, and preparing for the challenges and opportunities ahead.

Consider a family displaced by catastrophic flooding, a child in a rural community without access to clean drinking water or lifesaving vaccines, or communities facing the growing health impacts of climate change. Behind every public health challenge are people, families, and communities whose lives and futures are at stake.

Now imagine the opportunities: preventing outbreaks before they spread, advancing the interconnected health of people, animals, and ecosystems through a One Health approach, and creating a healthier future for everyone, everywhere.

The public health professionals who will address these challenges, and turn these opportunities into reality, are sitting in classrooms today. But as AI transforms education, research, and public health practice, educators face a fundamental question: How do we rethink teaching and learning to prepare students for an AI-enabled workforce, especially when time, technical expertise, and resources are limited?

Imagine if a professor could upload an existing syllabus and, within minutes, discover evidence-informed ways to transform traditional coursework into interactive, AI-enabled learning experiences. That's what we've built with PHACT, the Public Health AI Curriculum Transformation Platform.

Powered by AWS, PHACT combines generative AI, trusted public health knowledge, and human-in-the-lead review to help educators reimagine teaching and learning while maintaining academic oversight.

And the potential impact extends far beyond one classroom. ASPPH represents more than 150 schools and programs of public health, connecting a community of over 103,000 faculty, staff, students, and academic leaders. With more than 18,000 undergraduate public health graduates annually across the United States, the opportunity to transform how we prepare the next generation of public health professionals is significant.

And the need extends well beyond academia. Approximately 239,000 professionals work in U.S. state and local governmental public health agencies, while the broader global health and care workforce exceeds 70 million people.

By empowering educators with responsible, scalable AI, PHACT can create a multiplier effect that reaches across institutions, generations of students, and ultimately the communities they serve.

One educator. Many students. Countless communities.

We're not replacing educators. We're empowering them to prepare the next generation of public health professionals to address today's challenges and shape tomorrow's opportunities. Because the next great public health breakthrough may begin in a classroom, and PHACT can help make it possible.
